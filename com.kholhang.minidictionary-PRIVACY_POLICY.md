The App is operated in connection with dictionary content, optional sign-in, contributions, and related services. By using the App, you agree to this policy together with any terms shown elsewhere in the App or on our website.

We use the information above to operate and improve the App, authenticate users, sync dictionary data, show advertisements where enabled, send optional notifications you allow, prevent abuse, and comply with law.
