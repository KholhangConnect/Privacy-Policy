**Bundle identifier:** `com.vannoivah.kcclabu`  
**Effective date:** 13 May 2026  
**Operator / contact:** kholhangconnect@gmail.com; vannoivah@gmail.com  

This policy applies only to the **KCC Labu** native application distributed for Apple iOS (and related TestFlight or App Store builds). References prefixed with **`KCC_Labu_ios`** identify this policy version for support and compliance correspondence.

---

## 1. Summary

KCC Labu is a hymnal application for Kuki Christian Church worship. **All user data is stored locally on your device.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It contains no advertisements, no analytics, no user accounts, and no tracking.

The app may open **system-provided** interfaces (Mail, Safari, the App Store, or the share sheet) only when you explicitly choose to contact support, read legal terms, share a hymn, or visit a link.

---

## 2. Data we process and why

### 2.1 Data stored on your device (local only)

The app uses on-device storage to provide its features. This data **never leaves your device** unless you explicitly use a sharing or email feature.

| Feature | Storage method | What is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Hymn content | Core Data + bundled JSON files | Hymn lyrics, titles, and book metadata |
| Collections | Core Data | User-created groups of favourite hymns |
| Setlists | Core Data | User-created ordered lists of hymns for worship services |
| Recently viewed hymns | UserDefaults | A short list of recently opened hymn identifiers |
| User preferences | UserDefaults | Text size, typeface, appearance (light/dark), lyric layout, last-seen version |

**None of this data is transmitted to any server, third party, or other device.**

### 2.2 Sharing hymns

When you use **"Share Hymn"**, the app opens the iOS system share sheet with the hymn's lyrics as plain text. The app does not control or log where you send this text — that is governed by the receiving app (Messages, WhatsApp, email, etc.) and its own privacy policy.

### 2.3 Optional remote hymn updates (`RemoteHymnalBaseURL`)

If a **RemoteHymnalBaseURL** value is configured in the app's build, the app may **download** hymn or configuration files over **HTTPS** from that server. In that case:

- The operator of that server may receive **standard server logs** (IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested paths).
- The app does **not** transmit your name, email, Apple ID, or any personal identifier to that server.

If **RemoteHymnalBaseURL** is empty (as it is in the current App Store build), this network path is **not used** and the app makes **no outbound network requests** for core functionality.

### 2.4 Email and support

When you use **"Report an Issue"**, your device opens your **default mail app** with a pre-filled recipient address and subject line. **No email is sent until you choose to send it yourself.** Any personal data you include in that message is governed by your email provider and the recipient's handling of your correspondence.

### 2.5 Links and third-party sites

The app may open external websites in Safari when you tap certain links (for example the App Store listing, the project website, or the privacy policy). Those sites have **their own** privacy policies. This policy does not govern them.

The app opens external URLs only in response to **your explicit tap** — never automatically or in the background.

## 3. Data we do not collect

The app does **not**:

- Create or require a user account.
- Collect, store, or transmit personal information (name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers).
- Include any advertising or analytics SDKs.
- Use cookies, fingerprinting, or any form of tracking.
- Sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
- Access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device capability.

## 4. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience, including families and children. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, there is no special children's data to protect. If you believe otherwise, contact us at the addresses above and we will investigate promptly.

---

## 5. Security and retention

We rely on **Apple's platform security** (app sandboxing, data protection, and encryption at rest) for the app binary and on-device data. Data on your device remains until you delete the app or clear app data through iOS settings. No data is retained on any server operated by us unless you voluntarily send an email.

## 6. Your choices

- **Delete data:** Delete the app to remove all locally stored data (subject to iOS backup behaviour).
- **Sharing:** You choose when and whether to share hymn text. No sharing happens without your action.
- **Email:** You choose when and whether to send a support email. No email is composed or sent without your action.
- **Contact us:** You may contact us at the email addresses above. Please include **`KCC_Labu_ios`** in the subject line if you are writing about this privacy policy.
