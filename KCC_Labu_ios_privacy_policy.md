## 1. Summary

KCC Labu is a hymnal application. Most hymn and library data used in normal operation is **stored on your device**. The app may open **system** interfaces (for example Mail, Safari, or the App Store) when you choose to contact support, read legal terms, or share links. **Subscription purchases**, if offered, are processed **entirely by Apple**; this developer does not receive your full payment card details.

---

## 2. Data we process and why

### 2.1 Data stored on your device (local)

The app uses on-device storage (including Core Data and bundled or downloaded hymn content) to provide hymn browsing, collections, and related features. This data **remains on your device** unless you use a feature that explicitly sends information elsewhere (for example email or optional remote sync—see below).

### 2.2 Optional remote hymn updates (`RemoteHymnalBaseURL`)

If a **RemoteHymnalBaseURL** value is configured in the app’s build or settings, the app may **download** hymn or configuration files over **HTTPS** from that server. In that case:

- The operator of that server may receive **standard server logs** (for example IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested paths).
- The app does **not** intentionally transmit your name, email, or Apple ID to that server as part of this sync unless you separately provide it (for example by contacting the operator by email).

If **RemoteHymnalBaseURL** is empty or unset, this network path is not used.

### 2.3 In-app purchases and subscriptions

If subscription or other in-app products are enabled, purchase flows use **Apple StoreKit** and Apple’s systems. Apple’s privacy practices and terms apply to that processing. This developer typically receives only information Apple provides to developers about transactions (for example product type and subscription status), not your card number.

### 2.4 Email and support

When you use **“Report an issue (email)”** (or similar), your device opens your **default mail app** with a pre-filled recipient and subject. **No email is sent** until you send it yourself. Any personal data in that message is governed by your email provider and the recipient’s handling of your message.

### 2.5 Links and third-party sites

The app may open websites (for example the App Store, Apple’s Standard EULA, a project website, or GitHub) in Safari or an in-app browser. Those sites have **their own** privacy policies. This policy does not control them.

### 2.6 Google / Firebase configuration

The project may include a **GoogleService-Info.plist** for optional Firebase-related features. Unless and until you ship a build with Firebase services enabled and configured, **no Firebase-backed analytics or messaging** is implied by this policy. If you later enable Firebase products, you should **update this document** and your App Store privacy answers to list the Firebase data categories you actually use.

## 3. Data we do not intentionally collect

- Sell your personal information.
- Run third-party advertising SDKs in the reference configuration described above.
