# com.kholhang.multiquiz – Privacy Policy (Kholhang Multi QUIZ)

**Effective date:** May 2, 2026

`com.kholhang.multiquiz` (“Kholhang Multi QUIZ”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is a quiz app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – Information We Collect

- **No account data**: The app does not require sign-in and does not collect names, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **Quiz content data**: The app downloads quiz questions from **Firebase Realtime Database**. This is app content, not personal data.
- **Device data**: We do not intentionally collect device identifiers (such as advertising ID) or precise location.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – How We Use Information

- **To provide the app**: Fetch quiz questions from Firebase and show them in the app.
- **Offline access**: The app stores downloaded quiz questions on your device using an offline database (Room) so you can view/play the quiz without internet.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – Data Storage

- **On-device storage**: Quiz questions may be saved locally on your phone for offline use.
- **Firebase**: Quiz content is retrieved from Firebase Realtime Database when internet is available.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – Permissions

The app may request:

- **Internet / Network state**: To download quiz questions and detect connectivity.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – Data Sharing

- We **do not sell** personal information.
- We **do not share** personal information with third parties because we do not collect it.
- Quiz content is hosted on Firebase; access to it is required to load questions when online.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – Children’s Privacy

The app is intended as a general-audience quiz app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## com.kholhang.multiquiz – Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.
