## 1. Summary

Pathen Lekhabu Theng is a Bible reading application. **All user data is stored locally on your device.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It contains no advertisements, no analytics, no user accounts, and no tracking.

The app may open **system-provided** interfaces (Mail, Safari, the App Store, or the share sheet) only when you explicitly choose to contact support, read legal terms, share a verse, or visit a link.

---

## 2. Data we process and why

### 2.1 Data stored on your device (local only)

The app uses on-device storage to provide its features. This data **never leaves your device** unless you explicitly use a sharing or email feature.

| Feature | Storage method | What is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Bible text | Bundled data files | Scripture text, book names, chapter and verse metadata |
| Bookmarks / Highlights | Local database or UserDefaults | User-marked verses and highlight colours |
| Reading history | UserDefaults | Last-read book, chapter, and verse position |
| User preferences | UserDefaults | Text size, typeface, appearance (light/dark), and other display settings |

**None of this data is transmitted to any server, third party, or other device.**

### 2.2 Sharing verses

When you share a Bible verse, the app opens the iOS system share sheet with the verse text. The app does not control or log where you send this text — that is governed by the receiving app (Messages, WhatsApp, email, etc.) and its own privacy policy.

### 2.3 Email and support

When you use a **"Report an Issue"** or **"Contact Us"** feature, your device opens your **default mail app** with a pre-filled recipient address and subject line. **No email is sent until you choose to send it yourself.** Any personal data you include in that message is governed by your email provider and the recipient's handling of your correspondence.

### 2.4 Links and third-party sites

The app may open external websites in Safari when you tap certain links (for example the App Store listing, the project website, or the privacy policy). Those sites have **their own** privacy policies. This policy does not govern them.

The app opens external URLs only in response to **your explicit tap** — never automatically or in the background.

## 3. Data we do not collect

The app does **not**:

- Create or require a user account.
- Collect, store, or transmit personal information (name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers).
- Include any advertising or analytics SDKs.
- Use cookies, fingerprinting, or any form of tracking.
- Sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
- Access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device capability.

## 4. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience, including families and children. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, there is no special children's data to protect. If you believe otherwise, contact us at the addresses above and we will investigate promptly.

## 5. Security and retention

We rely on **Apple's platform security** (app sandboxing, data protection, and encryption at rest) for the app binary and on-device data. Data on your device remains until you delete the app or clear app data through iOS settings. No data is retained on any server operated by us unless you voluntarily send an email.
