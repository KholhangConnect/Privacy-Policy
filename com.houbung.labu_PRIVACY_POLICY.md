This Privacy Policy describes how Houbung Labu (“the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our Android application published by **Vannoivah**. By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Summary

Houbung Labu is a **free songbook** for KCC Labu and KBC Labu hymnals, developed as part of the [Vannoivah](https://vannoivah.wordpress.com/) community app project. We designed the app to keep most data **on your device**. We do **not** operate user accounts in the app, and we do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information stored on your device (not sent to our servers)

The app saves data locally using Android DataStore so your preferences and content work offline:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Display settings | Dark mode, font size, zoom, line spacing, layout options |
| Reading preferences | Verse labels, swipe navigation, keep screen on, etc. |
| Last opened songbook | Resume feature (if you enable “Remember last songbook”) |
| Favorites | Hymns you mark as favorites |
| History | Recently viewed hymns |
| Events (“My Events”) | Event names and song lists you create |
| Optional payment config | Saved only if present in app settings (e.g. custom payment link overrides) |

This information stays on your phone or tablet unless you use a system backup (see Section 5).

We **do not** collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location through the app itself.

### 2.2 Information you choose to share

When you use **Share**, the app sends hymn text or event lists through **other apps you select** (e.g. messaging or email). We do not control how those services handle your data.

### 2.3 Voluntary donations

If you choose to donate:

- **Google Play:** Payment is processed by **Google Play Billing**. Google collects payment and account information according to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Razorpay:** Payment may open **Razorpay** in the browser or in-app checkout. Razorpay processes payment data according to [Razorpay’s Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/).

We do not receive or store your full payment card or bank details. We may receive limited transaction-related information from payment providers as allowed by their policies (e.g. confirmation that a purchase occurred).

Donations are **voluntary** and do **not** unlock app features or content. For support or donation questions, contact us at **vannoivah@gmail.com**.

### 2.4 Internet access

The app requests **INTERNET** permission to:

- Open payment pages (e.g. Razorpay payment links) when you choose to donate  
- Load external content only when required for payments you initiate  

Hymn and songbook content included in the app is stored in the app package and does not require the internet for normal reading.

### 2.5 What we do not collect

As of this policy, the app does **not** include:

- User registration or login  
- Analytics or advertising SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, AdMob)  
- Crash reporting services we operate  
- Collection of contacts, photos, microphone, or location  

If we add such features in a future version, we will update this policy before or when they are released.

## 3. How we use information

We use locally stored data only to:

- Provide songbook browsing, search, lyrics display, favorites, history, and events  
- Remember your settings and optional resume position  
- Process voluntary donations you initiate through third-party payment providers  

We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- **On-device data** remains until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or reset the device.  
- **Android backup:** If device backup is enabled (`android:allowBackup` is true), your system may include app data in encrypted device backups according to your device and Google account settings.  
- **Payment records** are retained by Google or Razorpay under their policies.

**To delete local app data:** Android Settings → Apps → Houbung Labu → Storage → **Clear data**, or uninstall the app.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact **vannoivah@gmail.com** and we will take appropriate steps.
