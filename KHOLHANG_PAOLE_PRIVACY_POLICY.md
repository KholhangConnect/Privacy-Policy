# Privacy Policy — Kholhang Paole

**Last updated:** April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Kholhang Paole** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use our dictionary application for Android (the “**App**”).

---

## 1. Who this applies to

The App is provided in connection with dictionary content, optional sign-in, optional contributions and validation workflows, learning features, and related online services. By using the App, you agree to this policy together with any other terms or notices shown in the App, on Google Play, or on our website.

---

## 2. Information the App may process

### 2.1 Dictionary and learning data

- Search queries, favorites, history, and similar usage needed to operate the dictionary.
- Learning/practice activity you choose to use inside the App.
- Much of this is stored **on your device** so the App can work offline after you have synced dictionary data while online.

### 2.2 Account and authentication data

- If you use **Sign in with Google**, Google provides identifiers and profile information according to your Google account settings and Google’s policies (for example, user ID and email address used for contributor or validator features).
- We use this to identify your account on our backend where those features are enabled.

### 2.3 Contributions and moderation

- Text and metadata you submit as **dictionary contributions** (for example word, meaning, notes) may be stored on **servers** so administrators and validators can review, approve, or reject submissions.
- Related records may include timestamps, status, and administrative notes associated with moderation.

### 2.4 Technical and service data

When you use online features, push notifications, remote configuration, or ads, the App and our service providers may process data such as:

- Device or app identifiers useful for security, diagnostics, or delivery of content  
- **IP address** and connection timestamps  
- **Crash or diagnostic** data (as implemented in your build)  
- **Approximate analytics** (if enabled for your build)

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the App and dictionary services  
- Authenticate users and sync data  
- Operate contribution and validation workflows  
- Show **advertisements** where the App is configured to do so  
- Send **optional push notifications** when you allow them (for example dictionary or announcement messages)  
- Protect against abuse and comply with applicable law
