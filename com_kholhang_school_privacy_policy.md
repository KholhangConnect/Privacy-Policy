# Privacy Policy

**Application package:** `com.kholhang.school`  
**Application name:** Kholhang Smart School  
**Effective date:** 13 June 2026  
**Last updated:** 13 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Kholhang Smart School** (Android application ID `com.kholhang.school`) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use the school portal mobile app.

## 1. Who we are

Kholhang Smart School is a school management client that connects parents, learners, educators, and school staff to academic and administrative information hosted by your school. Your school is the primary data controller for learner and family records. The app operator provides the software client only.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information:** email address, username, display name, role, and authentication tokens.
- **Learner profile data:** name, class, section, roll number, and linked guardian relationships.
- **Academic records:** homework, assignments, attendance, examinations, grades, syllabus progress, behaviour notes, and timeline entries.
- **Financial information:** fee invoices and payment status. Payment card details are processed by third-party payment gateways; this app does not store full card numbers.
- **Documents and uploads:** photos or files you submit for homework, leave requests, or school documents.
- **Device and technical data:** device identifiers for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), app version, network status, and optional advertising identifiers when ads are enabled.
- **Usage data:** basic app interactions needed to operate features, sync offline changes, and improve reliability.

## 3. How we use information

- Authenticate users and provide role-based access to school modules.
- Display academic, attendance, fee, transport, library, and communication data from your school backend.
- Send homework, fee, and general school notifications when you allow notifications.
- Queue and sync offline create, update, and delete actions when connectivity returns.
- Show optional advertisements to eligible users who have provided consent, in compliance with Google Play policies.
- Respond to support, security, and account deletion requests.

## 4. Permissions

The app may request the following Android permissions:

- **Internet and network state** — connect to your school portal and cloud services.
- **Notifications** — deliver school alerts and reminders.
- **Camera** — capture documents or homework uploads when you choose to attach a photo.
- **Biometric / fingerprint** — optional faster sign-in where supported.
- **Advertising ID** — only when optional ads are enabled and permitted by policy.

## 5. Where data is stored

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored using Firebase and your school's configured backend services. Some session preferences and pending offline actions are stored locally on your device until synced.

## 6. Sharing with third parties

- **Google Firebase** — authentication, database, messaging, and hosting infrastructure.
- **Google Mobile Ads** — optional advertising when enabled and allowed.
- **Payment processors** — such as Razorpay or school-configured gateways for fee payments.
- **Your school** — administrators and authorized staff who manage portal data.

We do not sell personal information.

## 7. Children's privacy

The app may be used by learners under 18 under parental or school supervision. Personalized advertising is disabled by default for learner profiles. Schools and parents should review module access and consent settings in the app.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as your school account remains active or as required by your school and applicable law. You may request account deletion from **Settings → Delete account** in the app. Deletion requests are processed according to your school's policy and applicable regulations.

## 9. Your choices

- Manage notification permissions in Android system settings.
- Review ads and analytics consent in app Settings.
- Contact your school administrator to correct or access learner records.
- Sign out to end the active session on your device.

## 10. Security

We use encrypted transport, secure session storage, and platform security features. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please protect your login credentials.

## 11. International transfers

Cloud service providers may process data in data centres outside your country. Your school and service providers apply appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy for the `com.kholhang.school` application. Material changes will be reflected in the app and in the effective date above.

## 13. Contact

For data access, correction, or privacy questions, contact your school administrator first. For app-related privacy enquiries, email [privacy@kholhang.school](mailto:privacy@kholhang.school).
